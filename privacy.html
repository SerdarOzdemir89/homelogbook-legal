Privacy Policy

Loghaven for iOS · Effective 28 October 2026

Loghaven is built so that your home records stay on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

What the app stores

Items, purchase dates, warranty periods, service records, maintenance tasks, notes and the photos you attach are saved in a local database on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. They are not uploaded to us. We cannot read them.

Receipt scanning

When you pick a receipt photo, the text is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The image and the recognised text are not sent anywhere. The photo is stored with the item so you can show it later.

Notifications

Warranty and maintenance reminders are scheduled locally with Apple's UserNotifications framework. They are generated on your device; no push server is involved.

Purchases

Loghaven Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Apple provides the app with a purchase receipt so Pro features can be unlocked. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Data we collect

None. The App Store privacy label for Loghaven is "Data Not Collected".

Exporting and deleting

You can export a PDF home file at any time and share it wherever you choose. Deleting an item removes its records and documents from the device. Settings → Delete all records removes everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
