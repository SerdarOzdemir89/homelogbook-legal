Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no server. Everything is stored on your iPhone.

What does Pro unlock?

Unlimited items (the free version keeps five), warranty reminders 30 days before cover ends, and the PDF home file export. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Where is my data if I change phones?

Records live on the device. Before switching, open Settings → Home file and share the PDF to keep a copy. A device-to-device transfer with an encrypted backup carries the app data across.

The receipt scan read the wrong total

Scan quality decides the result. Every field is editable before saving; correct the total and continue. Nothing is saved silently.

I deleted an item by mistake

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone inside the app. The PDF export is the backup to keep.
