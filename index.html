Loghaven

The memory your house does not have. Every appliance, its warranty end date, its service history and the next maintenance task — on your iPhone, without an account.

What it does

  • Room-by-room inventory: brand, model, serial number, purchase date, price.
  • Warranty countdown and a reminder 30 days before cover ends.
  • Service history with provider, cost and notes.
  • Forty maintenance intervals, from HVAC filters to smoke detectors.
  • A PDF home file for insurance, a sale, or a technician.
  • Receipt scanning with Apple's Vision framework, on device.

What it does not do

No account, no bank link, no ads, no analytics, no subscription. Loghaven Pro is a one-time purchase.
